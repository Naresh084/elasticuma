Design a replicated, bounded-memory event queue in Go using only the standard library. Producers append ordered events, consumers acknowledge offsets, and a crashed node must recover from a write-ahead log. Include backpressure, checksums, segment compaction, leader failover assumptions, and deterministic replay. Explain safety and liveness invariants, then provide a compact implementation skeleton and tests for torn records, duplicate delivery, slow consumers, and recovery after compaction.
